# Fast Fashion Supply Chain Analytics

## Overview
This project examines a fast-fashion supply chain to understand how demand patterns, inventory decisions, and logistics network design affect operational performance and cost. The analysis applies quantitative supply chain and analytics techniques to evaluate trade-offs and support managerial decision-making.

## Business Context
Fast-fashion supply chains operate under short product lifecycles, demand uncertainty, and tight margins. Decisions related to forecasting, inventory planning, warehouse placement, and transportation directly impact service levels, costs, and overall efficiency. This project analyzes these decisions in an integrated manner using a simulated fast-fashion dataset.

## Scope of Analysis
The project focuses on the following areas:
- Product-level analysis of sales, revenue, profit, and returns  
- Demand pattern analysis and forecasting for selected products  
- Inventory planning using EOQ, safety stock, and reorder point calculations  
- Facility location analysis to evaluate warehouse placement options  
- Transportation and logistics cost analysis using optimization techniques  

## Key Takeaways
- Product-level profitability, returns, and demand variability play a key role in inventory and production planning decisions  
- Inventory planning techniques such as EOQ, safety stock, and reorder points help balance service levels with holding and ordering costs  
- Facility location and transportation decisions significantly influence overall logistics efficiency and cost structure  
- An integrated view of forecasting, inventory, and network design helps surface operational trade-offs and improvement opportunities in fast-fashion supply chains  

## Deliverables
- Python-based analytical workflows covering forecasting, inventory planning, and network optimization  
- A consulting-style report detailing data, methodology, analysis, and managerial recommendations
